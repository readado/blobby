# Blobby prototype art notes

The raster artwork was generated with the built-in image generation tool and then bundled into the Xcode asset catalog.

## Prompt set

All assets use the same direction: polished hand-painted 2D children's storybook game illustration, softly dimensional, organic texture, cool deep-sea rim lighting, clean mobile-game silhouettes, no text, and no watermark.

- **Blobby expression sheet:** The same cute pink glossy blobfish in four equal transparent quadrants—neutral floating, happily chewing, frightened with wide eyes, and tucked/compressed hiding—consistent proportions and right-facing angle.
- **Revised eating expression:** One happy pink Blobby with a clean closed smile and exactly three small translucent pale-blue bubbles beside his mouth; no food, green material, drool strands, slime, scenery, or background.
- **Food sheet:** Four equal transparent quadrants containing a pink deep-sea shrimp, orange-red crab, purple sea urchin, and golden-cream scallop shellfish, recognizable and non-anthropomorphic.
- **Predator:** One broad-headed, blue-black deep-sea predatory fish in left-facing side profile, subtle turquoise rim light and amber eye, imposing but family friendly, transparent background.
- **Anglerfish:** One left-facing deep-sea anglerfish with a broad indigo body, visible teeth, and a curved turquoise glowing lure; family friendly, isolated with real transparency.
- **Sixgill shark:** One left-facing bluntnose sixgill shark with a heavy slate-blue body, six subtle gill slits, closed mouth, and restrained turquoise rim light; isolated with real transparency.
- **Shelter:** An isolated rocky coral outcrop with a central cozy crevice, red soft coral, cup corals, tube sponges, anemones, and restrained colorful accents, transparent background.
- **Background:** A portrait 9:19.5 midnight-blue deep-ocean scene with open central water, faint rock silhouettes, light shafts, marine snow, and a rolling seafloor; no animals or UI.
- **App icon:** A close friendly portrait of Blobby against deep navy water with a restrained coral suggestion, simple and readable at small sizes, opaque square artwork without baked-in rounded corners.
- **Game Over portrait:** One front-facing sad Blobby looking directly at the viewer with a large droopy nose and classic downturned blobfish frown; sorrowful but cute, uninjured, isolated with real transparency.
